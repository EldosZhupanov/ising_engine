## Standing — proofs

- **Gauge Degeneracy.** For field-free Ising, E(s) = E(−s) exactly, so
  ⟨sᵢ⟩ ≡ 0 at every site and temperature. MaxCut is field-free (QUBO linear
  terms cancel in spin coordinates). *Corollary:* any algorithm whose state is
  per-variable marginals is **provably vacuous** here — mean-field annealing,
  UMDA, product-form EDAs, marginal-propagating BP. Not weak; vacuous.
- **Representation Invariance.** An algorithm on R(x), for R a bijection or a
  quotient by symmetry group G, gains **≤ log₂|G| bits**. For Z₂ that is
  **≤ 1 bit, independent of n**. No change of representation alters complexity
  class.
- **E = 2V − |E|** on unweighted instances — see
  [OPEN_PROBLEMS.md](OPEN_PROBLEMS.md) §1.

## RC-006 — the gradient-ledger invariant (Vector 1, VERIFIED)

`SpinState` maintains two ledgers updated independently on every flip:
`energies[rep]`, and the field ledger behind `delta_e_into`. They are
algebraically linked, and the engine never declares or uses the link.

    E = offset + L(x) + ( A_tot + Σⱼ dⱼxⱼ − 2·L(x) − Σᵢ ΔEᵢ ) / 4

with `A_tot = Σaᵢ` and weighted degrees `dⱼ = Σᵢ q_ij` as per-instance constants.
After a one-off O(E) setup the check is **O(n) per replica, no edge traversal** —
versus `audit()`'s O(E) per call.

**Verified bit-exact** (worst residual 0.000000e0) across 200 checks on 5
structurally distinct instances: biqmac `be100.1.sparse` (145 distinct weights,
−100…100), G1, G11/G32 (toroidal ±1), G22 (dense), and a 3D lattice spin glass —
on **both** backends, and under 200 sequential flips. Permanent guard:
`tests/test_gradient_ledger_invariant.rs` (3 tests).

**Limitation, stated so it is not over-trusted:** a *scalar aggregate*, so a
**necessary but not sufficient** condition — two compensating ΔEᵢ errors cancel
in the sum. It does not replace `audit()`; it is affordable at a frequency
`audit()` is not.

## RC-007 — the operator monoid is effectively commutative (Vector 3)

Swapping two operators changes the result **no more than changing the seed**.
Measured `rho = d_order/d_seed` over all 28 pairs of 8 operators, 6 instances ×
6 seeds: **28/28 have rho < 1.4**, max 1.32 (greedy × random_flip). My
pre-registered prediction — large rho for asymmetric pairs, since the schedule
should be dominated by whichever operator runs last — was **refuted**.

**Instrument confound, caught and corrected:** the first pass reported 57%
commuting with 8 `inf` rows, because from the all-zeros init every replica is
identical, so `replica_exchange` and `houdayer_cluster` are **inert** and
deterministic operators have *zero seed variance* (rho divides by zero). Re-run
with a randomising sweep prepended → 100% commuting, zero `inf`. The instrument
had inherited the RC-002 defect.

Genuine exact commutation survives the correction:
`greedy_descent × replica_exchange` and `steepest_descent × replica_exchange`
have `d_order` = exactly 0 against a non-zero null — deterministic descent acts
independently per replica, so permuting and descending commute provably.

**Consequence:** the Evolution Engine's ordering search explores distinctions
that largely do not exist at pair level — a mechanism for the recorded result
that evolved plans lose 5/5 to `UltimateSolver`. **Pairs only**; longer schedules
untested. Unfixed (narrowing the search space is trajectory-changing).
Record: `../research/RC007_OPERATOR_COMMUTATIVITY.md`.

## RC-008 — ensemble collapse at T=0.1; RC-003's premise audited

Two-chain coupling diagnostic (independent inits, independent RNG, flat ladders).
**At T=0.1 the ensemble collapses completely: q_AA = 1.0000 exactly** — all 16
replicas become one configuration, the two chains stay 0.22–0.24 apart, energies
never agree, unchanged 200→800 sweeps. Broken ergodicity; "ensemble statistics"
there are statistics of a single point. **At T ≥ 0.5 chains mix by 200 sweeps**
(q_AB indistinguishable from q_AA, energies within 2σ). Crossover between 0.1
and 0.5.

**Audit outcome:** RC-003's ergodic premise was **sound where applied** (its
sweeps were mostly T ≥ 0.5) — a scope caveat, not a refutation. **RC-002 is
reconciled:** its "diversity persists to temp_hi=4.0" used PT *ladders* spanning
down to 0.1, so every run always contained cold non-mixing replicas. The ladder
preserved the memory. Flat ladders mix at the same nominal temperature. Both
correct, different objects.

**Instrument fix mid-cycle:** first pass said "not mixed" everywhere — I had used
an absolute energy threshold (1e-3) *below* the sampling noise of a 16-replica
mean. Replaced with a 2σ bound on the difference of two means. Residual
MIXED/not-mixed flicker at T≥0.5 is R=16 noise and is not reported as signal.
One instance, one operator. Record: `../research/RC008_MIXING_AND_ENSEMBLE_COLLAPSE.md`.

## RC-009 — capability passport vs reality (Vector: state representation) — CONFIRMATORY

The generative principle behind RC-005/007/008, stated by the user: *an optimizer
contains internal models (cost, mixing, operator equivalence, state
representation); discrepancies between model and behaviour are where new
algorithms live.* Three were done; **state representation** was the untouched one.

The engine's model of state representation is the **capability passport**, and
ADR-0001 makes it load-bearing (selection is by capability, never by name). All
18 operators declare `supports_sparse: true` and `supports_dense: true`.

**Verification gap found:** `tests/test_dense_byte_verification.rs` has exactly
two tests — one checking the DenseByte *backend primitives* against the f64
oracle, one timing. It exercises **zero operators**. So "every operator runs
correctly on DenseByte" was a universal claim about state representation that
nothing verified.

**Gap closed, no discrepancy found.** New suite
`tests/test_operator_backend_passport.rs` (2 tests): for all 18 operators on every
backend their passport claims — no panic, `audit()` drift **0.0**, energies
**identical** to the oracle. Also verifies `needs_replicas: false` is accurate by
running those operators at R=1. All pass.

**Non-vacuity guarded explicitly.** Cross-backend agreement is worthless if the
operators did nothing, so the suite records a post-warm-up baseline and *fails* if
half the operators are inert. Result: **0 of 18 inert** — every operator changed
the energy, so the agreement is real evidence. (Both prior cycles were bitten by
vacuity: RC-007's inert replica operators, and the retracted Law 2's empty control
group.) A randomising warm-up is applied first so replica-coupled operators have
genuine diversity to act on.

**Outcome: confirmatory.** The passport model matches reality. Recorded because an
unverified universal claim is now verified — and because a negative search result
is still a result.

## RC-011 — predictor's 0.747 provably cannot measure transfer (a PROOF)

`ROADMAP.md` cited "predictor leave-one-instance-out Spearman +0.747" as evidence
cross-family transfer is real. **It is mathematically incapable of that.**

`predictor::feature_row` = `[bias | instance features | schedule features | op
indicators]` with **no interaction terms** ⇒ purely additive
`ŷ = w·x_inst + w·x_sched`. `evaluate_predictor` scores each fold over rows of ONE
held-out instance, which all share that signature ⇒ `w·x_inst` is a **constant
offset** ⇒ Spearman (rank-based) is **provably invariant** to the instance
features and all their weights. A predictor ignoring the instance scores the same.

**Empirically confirmed on the real 18,570-row DB:** `predict(sigA,s)−predict(sigB,s)`
max deviation **1.110e-16** (machine ε) over 7 very different schedules; **0**
signature violations across 23 instances; metric reproduced at **0.7467**/23 folds.

**Sound part:** the fold structure is correct — `leave_one_instance_out` excludes
the whole instance, so there is no row-level leakage despite ~800 rows/instance.

**NOT claimed:** the predictor is fine — ranking schedules is exactly what
`filter` uses it for. **The portability 9/12 claim is separate and untouched.**
ROADMAP corrected in place. To make the metric transfer-sensitive you must add
instance×schedule interactions, or score across folds (e.g. predicted-vs-actual
instance-mean improvement, where instance features actually vary).
Record: `../research/RC011_PREDICTOR_METRIC_INVARIANCE.md`.

## Method rules these cycles produced

- Embed the null **inside** the algorithm (λ=0 ⇒ provably the baseline,
  unit-tested bit-identical) so construction error cannot be mistaken for effect.
- Claiming "no difference" requires an **equivalence bound**, not p > 0.05.
- Rank **scale-free relative differences**, not raw energies — a pooled Wilcoxon
  on raw energies across heterogeneous instances is dominated by the largest
  graphs (this changed one p from 0.107 to 1.3e-3).
- Check the control group is non-empty before interpreting any grouped comparison.
- When timing on this host, **interleave conditions within each repetition and
  take the median** — sequential blocks let ~9% monotonic drift masquerade as a
  monotonic effect.
- **Audit a controller at its deployed configuration, not its unit-test one.**
  RC-012's flag passed its module test yet cannot activate in deployment (row
  floor); and "fires with predicted=0.0000 at exactly min_frac, every time" was
  the tell that the learned gate was doing no work.
- **Ask what a metric is mathematically capable of detecting, before trusting what
  it is cited as evidence for.** RC-011: a rank-based score over a fold whose
  features are constant is provably blind to those features, no matter how good
  the number looks.
- **Check whether a reported metric was measured in-sample.** RC-010 found 4 of 5
  "held-out" candidates were verbatim training members, worth ~0.08 of Spearman.
- **Derive attainability grids only after checking tie handling.** RC-010's first
  forensic step wrongly excluded n=5 by assuming untied Spearman.
- **A threshold must be calibrated against its own noise floor.** RC-008's first
  pass reported "not mixed" at every temperature because the energy criterion was
  an absolute constant below the sampling error of a 16-replica mean. Compare to
  the standard error, never to a hard-coded epsilon.
- **Check your instrument for the defects you already documented.** RC-007's
  first pass was confounded by the all-zeros init that RC-002 had established two
  cycles earlier; exact zeros and `inf` in a ratio are the tell.
- A fitted term is only earned if it is **inert where theory says it should be**.
  RC-005's `min(φ,1/3)` cap changes nothing on instances that never reach the
  threshold and lifts R² only on those that cross it; a term that improved every
  fit equally would be an extra parameter, not a discovered mechanism.
