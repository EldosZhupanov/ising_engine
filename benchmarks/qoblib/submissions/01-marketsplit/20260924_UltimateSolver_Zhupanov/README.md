## Technical Methodology

1. **Exact QUBO Formulation:**
   We formulate the Market Split equality system $Ax = b$ as an unconstrained binary quadratic Hamiltonian:
   $$\min_{x \in \{0, 1\}^n} H(x) = \sum_{k=1}^m \left( \sum_{j=1}^n A_{kj} x_j - b_k \right)^2 \ge 0$$
   $H(x) = 0$ if and only if all $m$ subset-sum equations are simultaneously and exactly satisfied.

2. **Algorithmic Engine:**
   - **Parallel Tempering:** Multi-replica geometric temperature ladder with exact Metropolis exchange.
   - **CD005 Edge 2-Opt Escapes:** Escapes rugged local traps with coordinated spin updates.
   - **Dynamic Tabu Polish:** Short-tenure recency prohibition and aspiration criterion guaranteeing zero-residual convergence.
