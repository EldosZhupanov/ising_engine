# H06: Frustration-cycle coordinates

Status: KNOWN duality; DERIVED adaptive patch decoding.

When costly information is concentrated in a sparse frustration syndrome, optimize domain-wall variables with cycle constraints instead of absolute spins.

## Distinction from this repository

ICM and bond clusters use spins/overlap; they do not optimize a cycle-syndrome representation. Weighted signed cycles avoid the unweighted violation-count tautology of RC-004.

## Prior art

[Nishimori & Sollich 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0405313); [Boettcher reductions](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.1941). These identify equivalent principles; published benchmark advantages are not adopted as verified facts. Absence of an exact search hit does not establish novelty.

## Falsifier

Archive if syndrome maintenance costs dominate or dense graphs increase dimension without reducing hard constraints; use nonplanar adversarial graphs.
