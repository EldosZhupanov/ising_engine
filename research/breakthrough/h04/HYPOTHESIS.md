# H04: Gauge-aligned basin-conditioned distributions

Status: DERIVED; effectiveness untested.

Conditioning on symmetry orbit and basin before estimating probabilities preserves relational structure erased by unconditional marginals.

## Distinction from this repository

Covariance cluster synthesis exists; it does not update or sample an explicit gauge-aligned mixture distribution. Gauge theorem does not prohibit this (GAUGE_SCOPE). hBOA/EDA prior art prevents a scientific novelty claim.

## Prior art

[Pelikan et al. hBOA spin glasses](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.2997); [multilevel correlations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07793). These identify equivalent principles; published benchmark advantages are not adopted as verified facts. Absence of an exact search hit does not establish novelty.

## Falsifier

Compare unaligned product, aligned product, aligned tree, mixture and random diversity controls. Reject if benefits are explained by random initialization or duplicate-basins bias.
